THE LAST TRANSMISSION

FADE IN:

INT. ORBITAL STATION KESTREL - COMMAND DECK - NIGHT

Red emergency lights pulse across a cramped, cluttered command
deck. CAPTAIN MAYA OKONKWO-REYES (40s, steel-eyed, a burn scar
tracing her jaw) grips a console, scanning a wall of failing
readouts.

                    CAPTAIN OKONKWO-REYES
          Talk to me, Whit. What are we
          looking at?

WHITAKER "WHIT" DOYLE (20s, wiry, perpetually anxious, headset
askew) doesn't look up from his terminal, fingers flying.

                    WHIT
          Hull breach, deck three. Not
          good, Captain. Not good at all.

An older man, DR. AUGUSTINE PELL (60s, silver-bearded, calm
in a way that unsettles people) steps in from the corridor,
still in a lab coat.

                    DR. PELL
          The reactor can hold. I designed
          it to hold. Maya, listen to me --
          we are not losing this station
          tonight.

                    CAPTAIN OKONKWO-REYES
          You said that about the last one,
          Augustine.

A beat. Pell says nothing.

In the doorway, ENSIGN TAMSIN VOSS (20s, nervous energy,
clutching a datapad like a shield) hovers, unsure whether to
enter.

                    ENSIGN VOSS
          Captain -- sorry -- Ground
          Control is asking for a status
          update. Should I tell them --

                    CAPTAIN OKONKWO-REYES
          Tell them we're handling it.

Voss nods quickly and retreats. A GROUND CONTROL VOICE
crackles over the comm, distorted and distant.

                    GROUND CONTROL (V.O.)
          Kestrel, this is Ground Control,
          please advise on reactor status,
          over.

Whit glances at Okonkwo-Reyes. She shakes her head -- not yet.

In the corner, a maintenance TECHNICIAN in a grease-streaked
jumpsuit works silently on an open panel, never speaking,
occasionally glancing at the chaos with weary resignation.

                    DR. PELL
          Maya. Trust me one more time.

She looks at him for a long moment.

                    CAPTAIN OKONKWO-REYES
          You have four minutes.

FADE OUT.
